About Us

Our history, vision, and values

Our History

Nifemi GenAlpha Private School was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide world-class education that blends academic excellence with strong moral values. Starting with just 50 students, we have grown into a vibrant community of over 1,000 learners across nursery, primary, and secondary levels.

Over the years, we have consistently produced outstanding results in national examinations and have earned a reputation for discipline, innovation, and holistic development.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a leading institution that raises global leaders who are intellectually sound, morally upright, and socially responsible.

Mission: To provide a nurturing and stimulating environment where every child is empowered to reach their full potential through quality teaching, modern facilities, and character development.

Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Excellence
  • Innovation
  • Respect
  • Responsibility

Why Choose Our School

  • Experienced and dedicated teachers
  • Modern classrooms with smart boards
  • Well-equipped science and ICT labs
  • Rich extracurricular programs
  • Safe and conducive learning environment
  • Strong pastoral care and counseling

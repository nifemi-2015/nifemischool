Admissions

Join the GenAlpha family

Admission Requirements

  • Birth certificate (for Nursery & Primary)
  • Previous school report (for transfer students)
  • Passport photographs (2 copies)
  • Entrance examination (for JSS & SSS)
  • Interview with parents/guardians

Admission Process

  1. Obtain application form online or at the school.
  2. Submit completed form with required documents.
  3. Take the entrance assessment (if applicable).
  4. Attend the family interview.
  5. Receive admission offer and pay fees.
